Our Story

Why "28 Days"?

Psychology says it takes 28 consecutive days to build a habit that sticks. In the world of specialty coffee, 28 days also marks the peak post-roast window when whole beans hold their most complex, vibrant aromas.

Founded by independent baristas and coffee enthusiasts, 28 Days was created to be the ritual you look forward to waking up for. From ethical single-origin farms to our slow-batch micro-roaster, each pour embodies patience, precision, and community warmth.

Purpose & Future

Our Mission & Vision

Guiding principles fueling every single cup we brew.

Our Mission

To transform ordinary daily routines into intentional moments of comfort and joy. We commit to transparent farmer partnerships, fair wages, zero compromises on bean freshness, and crafting consistent brews that nurture connection.

Our Vision

To build a mindful global coffee community where quality, environmental stewardship, and heartfelt hospitality thrive together—proving that small, mindful everyday rituals can shape a better world.
